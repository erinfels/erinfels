About me:

Erin Fels is a junior at the University of Colorado, Boulder. She is studying Creative Technology and Design and Computer Science with a minor in Business. Erin is passionate about themed entertainment and immersive technologies, with an interest in creating memorable, interactive experiences.
She is also the current Vice-President and past social media chair for CU’s Themed Park Engineering and Design Club, where she has competed in the Ride Engineering Competition and Toronto Metropolitan Thrill Design Competition Qualifier and a member of CU’s Engineering Honor Society, Tau Beta Pi. At CU’s Atlas Institute, Erin works as a Creative Technology and Design Ambassador and Learning Assistant for a physical computing class. This past summer, she gained hands on industry experience as a Design and Programming Intern at Image Engineering, working on live special effect shows. Erin is a hardworking and receptive person.
Erin is a hardworking, curious, and receptive to feedback. Her goal in the future is to build a career in themed entertainment, combining her creative and technical skills to help bring exciting experiences to life.
