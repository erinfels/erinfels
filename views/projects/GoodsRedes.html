Redesign of Goodreads

Redesigned an application that needed an updated UI/UX.

Overview:

For the Intro to Human-Computer Interaction class, we had to redesign an app or website of our choosing that we felt had poor UI/UX. I choose Goodreads. Features I added and layout choices I made were to bring the description closer to the top, add a "Did Not Finish" book section, and get rid of duplicate functions.
