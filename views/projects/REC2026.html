The Sunnyside Scrambler

The Sunnyside Scrambler, CU Theme Park Engingeering and Design's 2026 Ride Engineering Competition Entry

Overview:

The compeition prompt was to create a 1:50 scale flat ride for Jolly Rancher Chew "riders" that had to be dissasmbled and reassembled in a time limit and envelope. The scrambler egg and chicken themed scrambler was designed and documented in adherence with professional ride design standards including ASTM F2291. The team placed second in the west division out of 16 teams.

For the 2026 year, I was chosen to be our Theming Electrical Lead. In this position I helped decide, create and finalize design decision but also code and fabricate our electrical systems. My work included coding I2C interrupts that the safety related control systems ran off of and soldering many of our electircal componest and day of circuit set up. I was also the main graphic designer where I create the logo and poster while also facililating all design choices like the theme and what elements were included. In the theming category, the team placed 2nd in the west devision also.
