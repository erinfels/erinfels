The Sunnyside Scrambler

The Sunnyside Scrambler, CU Theme Park Engineering and Design's 2026 Ride Engineering Competition Entry

Overview:

The Sunnyside Scrambler was created for the 2026 Ride Engineering Competition. The competition prompt was to create a 1:50 scale flat ride for Jolly Rancher Chew "riders" that had to be disassembled and reassembled in a time limit and envelope. The scrambled egg and chicken themed scrambler was designed for the competition along with documentation on ASTM F2291, risk assessment, controls and mechanical design and Factory Assessment Tests and Site Acceptance Tests. The team placed second in the west division out of 16 teams.

My role:

For the 2026 year, I was chosen to be our Theming Electrical Lead. In this position I helped decide, create, and finalize design decisions to help create a cohesive and exciting experience for our riders. The theming is judged on the excitement, marketability and how captivating it is. I was also the main graphic designer where I create the logo and poster while also facilitating all design choices like the theme and what elements were included. In the theming category, the team placed 2nd in the west division also. I also assisted in writing of code and fabrication of our electrical systems. My work included coding I2C interrupts that the safety related control systems ran off of, soldering many of our electrical components and assembly and disassembly on competition day.
